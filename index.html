Sculpt Scan

Scan a real object and download it as a 3D model (OBJ) for sculpting, carving or 3D printing.

How to get a good scan
  • Put the object on a table with some space around it. Plain, matte objects scan best; shiny, glass or very dark ones scan poorly.
  • Tap the table next to the object, then tap the object. Shrink the box so it just fits.
  • Walk slowly all the way around, about an arm's length away, and look at it from above too. The ring fills in as each side is covered.
  • Hidden parts (like the underside) are filled in solid, so the model is closed and ready for sculpting or printing.

Your scan

Scans from a headset or phone capture the overall form (roughly 5–10 mm detail), not fine texture.
